Package 2.1506 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Time tracking: adding, editing or deleting an entry and starting or stopping the timer show at once (the dialog closes, the entry says "Saving…", sums "Calculating…"); no full reload of the app state after a write; a double click on "Start timer" starts one timer (#7501)
- Travel expenses and logbook: a second receipt or drive added while the first is still saving stays in the list; the next drive starts at the end odometer of the previous one, also before its answer; a failed save keeps the row with "Try again"; "Bill on" opens one dialog (#7503)
- Letters and Writing & contracts: autosave runs one save at a time (no conflict against the own previous save), the editor shows "Saving…" / "Saved" / "Not saved – Try again", buttons that save first show that they wait; the document keeps unsaved input in the browser and its preview follows the save (#7504)
- Own webhooks and forwardings (Slack, Matrix, Telegram, Signal) and the private mailbox's server for sending are treated as ways into the account: creating one or changing its target asks for the password and sends a security mail; a new password, a reset or the first second factor switch the webhooks and forwardings off ("switched off by the new password", also those already off after failures) (#7633)
- One list of every way into and out of an account (ACCESS_WAYS) drives what a new password ends; a test holds every table with a key and every column with an outward target against it (#7633)
- The webhook of a document flow in the private workspace, a new sending target of the private mailbox (server, port, user, or the first password after an import) and an agent's new webhook address ask for the password and are told by a security mail; a new password switches the flow's webhook off until the flow is saved again, while the mailbox's server and the agent's webhook stay - their mail says so and where to check them (#7633)

### Added
- Help: new areas Organisation, Company & office and Account - create your company, invite people and roles, set up the office, who sees what, coming from another program, e-invoices, account and forgotten password, help from a person; "How it works" in Settings > Organisation (#6535)

### Fixed
- The sign-in enrolment of a second factor forced by the admin ends older sessions and access ways like the first second factor, with a regression test, and the outdated comments on the reset link and the scan keys are corrected: findings F6 and F8 of #7446 built; F7 (calendar feed links) follows in #7736 (#7446)
