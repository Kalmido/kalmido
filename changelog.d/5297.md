### Added
- #6699 Invoice issuer keys (stage 1b of the platform billing): an admin of the organisation (in the private office its owner) sets up an invoice issuer for one additional number range in the office settings > Invoice issuers. Its key (`kis_test_…` / `kis_live_…` by `KALMIDO_ISSUER_ENV`, shown once, stored as a hash) is a kind of its own without a person: it reaches only `GET /api/v1/issuer` (name, environment, range, limits, tax profile), never `/api/office` or any other route. Review mode only. Limits with careful defaults (never unlimited), address rule (required for live keys), pause and self-pause after probing calls, keys of at most 365 days with a chosen overlap (at most 14 days) and at most two valid at once, bank accounts for later documents (sealed, never readable with the key), office log of every change and call, alarms to the area's admins (new key, new address, self-pause always by e-mail too), notices 30 / 7 / 0 days before a key runs out. Setting up, a new key, raising a limit, loosening the address rule, a bank account and resuming after a self-pause ask for the password. `KALMIDO_ISSUER_KEYS=0` switches the kind off; on a hosted server the operator unlocks each area (`PUT /api/operator/accounts/<id>` `issuer_keys`, locking pauses). Moving the private office into an organisation revokes its issuers, a restore of a backup pauses all of them. Migration 6699: tables `office_issuers`, `office_issuer_keys`, `office_issuer_payees`, `office_issuer_refs`, `office_issuer_ips`; columns `office_doc_log.issuer_id`, `orgs.issuer_keys` (additive).
