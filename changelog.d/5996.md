Package 2.1490.0 (Bündel: Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Added
- Core building block "write shows at once" for editors with rows (core.js `rwRows`, on `optWrite`; docs/dev/kern.md): a change stands at once, the saves run one at a time behind it, each built from the newest local state, waiting changes join one request; a row being saved dims its amount, a row that failed shows "Not saved" with "Try again"; one writer per document; the answer only brings what the server computes (sums, ids, version) and draws only the rows and sums, never the whole editor (#7493)

### Changed
- Quotation and invoice editor: "Add service", free position, discount line, text block, removing, moving and every field of a position show at once, also on a slow line - no waiting for the server; "Calculating…" stands next to "Positions" until the server's answer is there; typing in a line while an answer comes keeps the focus and the text; a save that fails or gets no connection leaves the line in place with "Not saved – Try again" at its end; a removed or moved line whose save is refused or gets no answer comes back to its place, said with "Try again"; a change made meanwhile by someone else still shows the conflict bar (#7493)
