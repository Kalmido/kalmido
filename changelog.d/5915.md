Package 2.1477 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- A new version reloads the app by itself only while the window or tab is hidden (and nothing is being typed, waiting or uploading); in view the "New version" bar stays and the next change of page or a click on the bar reloads (#6364).
- Office frame: a reload keeps the shown data on screen, dimmed and `aria-busy`, until the fresh answer is there; a failed fetch leaves the rows and shows the reason with "Try again"; the big loading block only appears on the very first load. One helper `ofcRefetch` (with sequence guard) replaces the hand-written reloads of subscriptions, agreements, cash flow, templates, meals, travel, logbook, cash book, cards, bank, receipts missing, pipeline, ledger, bookkeeping and letters; a change of filter or period shows the old state dimmed. The 4-second hold and tap replay are gone (#7495).
- Sections: project sections and own sections of My tasks share one head and one menu (Rename, Move up / down - in a board column Move left / right -, extras, Delete); what a section can do comes from the view's adapter (`DROP_ADAPTERS` `sec`). `tools/check_drop_core.py` also counts head special ways in render.js (#7519).
- Office PDF: on one-page documents with a block glued to the footer (bank line + GiroCode, payment block, signature lines) totals and closing move all the way down, so the free space is one gap above the totals; without such a block the 25 mm limit stays. The default reminder texts say "the bank account below" (#7523).

### Fixed
- Shared computer: without "Stay signed in" the browser keeps no offline state and no knowledge copy (`remember` in `/api/me` and `/api/state`); an offline start drops a state older than 14 days; a 401 clears everything except waiting changes, conflict copies and drafts, forgets the remembered keys and unsubscribes push locally; push goes only to subscriptions of a still valid session, and a new sign-in removes expired sessions together with their subscriptions (#6500).
