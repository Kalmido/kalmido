Package 2.1481 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- The general input (upload with sorting, share target, own document address, mailbox rule) keeps the office it is brought in for on the document (`doc_meta.area_to`); recognition no longer guesses it from the current setting. An invoice with no clear office stays visible in the own documents, and "New in the office" of every office that is on offers it to the uploader alone (#7325)
- One invoice recogniser for sorting and the move of old receipts: an invoice number with a net amount or VAT rate (or an e-invoice); a single sign no longer sorts into the office (#7325)
- The office's own receipt upload goes only into the office that is open (409 otherwise); the receipt fetcher refuses uploads (409) while the office is switched off for its person in that workspace (#7321)
- An agent writes only into an office that is on for the person it works for; otherwise it gets 409 `office_off` with a readable text, and its drafts' links name their office (`#office/s/<area>/...`) (#7326)
- Joining an organisation, links into another workspace's office (equipment, travel costs, office favourites) and resetting the sidebar no longer switch the workspace on top; a workspace changed on another device is announced once with "Back" (#7328)

### Fixed
- Uploaded invoices no longer vanish under "All workspaces" with several organisations, land in an organisation's office while working in the own one, or hide behind "Also private" switched off (#7325)
- Documents of the general input that were never sorted under "All workspaces" before this release stay where they are, visible in the own documents; there is no data run for them (#7325)
- An agent's draft no longer lands hidden in the own office while "Also private" is off, and its chat link no longer opens a 404 under another workspace (#7326)
- Audit #5704 F24: the four office points checked; the office upload and the receipt fetcher fixed, the other three closed with reasons on the task (#7321)
