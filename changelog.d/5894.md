Package 2.1472 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Added
- Account move: every server has a fixed random key in the export's manifest; the double-import guard follows it instead of the server's address (#6593)
- CI: tools/check_umz_v.py - UMZ_V must rise when a table of the account export loses or renames a column (#6593)

### Changed
- Lowering a member to guest, accounting or read-only hands their projects, calendars and address books in the organisation to the admin changing the role; they stay in them with edit (read-only: view). Guests and accounting have at most edit in projects and leave the office's rights groups; the role dialog names what goes over first (#6610)
- Office: small business (§ 19 UStG) and the VAT basis have no default for new offices and are chosen once in the office setup; existing offices keep cash basis. Small business has an "applies from" day: earlier periods are evaluated as before, and the finalising lock checks the invoice date. Switching asks first and names the open drafts (#6533)
- Account import: an export of a newer Kalmido version is refused on an own server with the version to update to; the hosted server names the unknown parts and fields in the preview (#6593)
- Account import: the double-import guard holds per target organisation (any of its admins), not per person (#6593)
- docs/UPGRADING.md: how far back in short, the tested starting points, "from 2.763 on, back only through a backup" instead of a paragraph per one-way step (#6616)
- The update hint (Administration > Server, admin alert) points to UPGRADING.md when more than one minor lies between (#6616)

### Fixed
- Moving a client out of an organisation: a member without the right gets the 403 before the hint about finalised documents (#7408)
- CI: tools/check_layout.py and tools/check_glossar.py treat the one-shot fragments of kalmido/core/oncesteps.d like migrations.d (data, not modules); the office one-shot of #6533 is the first such fragment (#6533)
- Account import: rows without a valid value in a required field are counted per area and named in the result instead of vanishing silently (#6593)
- Start of an install from before 2.37 (SQLite data/tasks.db next to an empty Postgres) names the way through 2.37.0 and 2.1001.0 instead of "point DATABASE_URL" (#6616)

No one-way step: #6616 changes no data; UPGRADING.md now states the existing ones in short (updates go directly from 2.1000 on, #6826; from 2.763 on going back works only through a backup made before the update).
