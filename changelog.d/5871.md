Package 2.1469 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Settings: Integrations and Access are one tab "Connections", always shown, in four groups with a symbol (Phone and calendar, E-mail, Other programs, Agents); the app passwords moved there from Account and "For advanced users" in Account is gone; old links to Access / Integrations land in Connections (#6368)
- "New" sits in one place: on a computer and in the tablet layout one blue button in the head bar next to the title, on a phone the round + named after what it creates (hold it for further kinds) - Notes, Files, Tables, Knowledge and Calendar first, the other areas follow (#6370)

### Fixed
- Family: two parents approving the same reward request no longer take the stars twice - the second gets "Already redeemed" and the list reloads (#6343)
- Habits: ticking the same day on two devices adds both steps instead of losing one (#6343)
- Dispatch board and absences: saving a booking or an absence no longer resets what another device changed meanwhile (period, status) - only the changed fields are saved, a conflict shows the merge bar, Undo takes back only this change (#6342)
- Folder sharing: the dialog's head names the real owner of the folder's projects instead of always showing the viewer as owner (#6679)
