Package 2.1489.0 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Office PDF: on one-page documents with a block glued to the footer (bank line + GiroCode, payment block, signature lines) totals and closing move all the way down, so the free space is one gap above the totals; without such a block the 25 mm limit stays. The default reminder texts say "the bank account below" (#7523).
- Decisions apply again (#6842): a new project in a folder with a project type starts without the standard sections unless ticked (#972); no film samples among the document templates, also with Film & Video (#2283; templates made from one stay); receipts upload on a computer is one button "Upload files", on a phone "Photograph receipt" and "Upload files" side by side, no sheet - the receipts list and the bookkeeping alike (#4892); the celebration figure is about a quarter of the width (#1292).
- Invoices (#7012, audit #5248 B1 B2 B9): an open finalised invoice has "Record payment…" in its row menu; "New invoice" has "Recurring invoice (series)…" (a new draft with the series sheet), the section "Invoice series" shows without a series too; the "…" of quotations and invoices has "Layout & numbers…" (Settings > Company & office); Documents & numbers has a "Signature…" button.

### Fixed
- Office start card "Where is your office?": the hint below "Basis of the income statement" explains the chosen basis (default Receipts) and follows the choice, with the same texts as in Settings (#7594).
