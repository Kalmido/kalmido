Package 2.1493 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher), Favoriten: Meine Aufgaben lässt sich nicht als Fav).

### Changed
- Chat pop-up (phone): the card for new chat messages sits on the finger while it is dragged (no transition, the pointer captured), the axis is fixed after the first pixels; past 60 px or with a flick it flies out sideways or up and is gone, short of it it springs back; down moves nothing. The end of a swipe still opens no chat; with reduced motion it goes at once (#7615).
- Phone drawer, area Start: under the area tiles two groups "Favourites" (the favourites of the row) and "Recently opened" (the source of the search's "Recently viewed", at most 5), plain rows that fold like the other groups; a tap opens the target and closes the drawer; an empty group is left out (#7617).

### Fixed
- Phone drawer head: the word mark sits on the middle of the bot sign (it stood 13.5 px too high: its box carried `.sbbt`, the class of the folder-tree rows, and took their row rules; now `.sbbw`), capitals about 45 % of the sign, less air between the head and the search; the area tiles keep their words apart ("Gewohnheiten") (#7616).
- Favourites: the own person page "My tasks" (#who/<me>) set as a favourite now shows its tile - the stored favourite was never drawn because the list of people leaves the own account out (#7525).
