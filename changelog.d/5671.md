Package 2.1430 (Bündel: Produkt- und UI-Regeln als docs/dev/produkt-regeln, Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher), Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohn).

### Added
- `docs/dev/produkt-regeln.md`: the product and UI rules every package keeps (navigation, touch and gestures, calm view, settings, look, words, the bell, agents and help, the freelancer principle, strict invoice fields), each with its day and tasks; the ones seen in the code text are new rules of `tools/check_rules.py`: `window-open` (a tab or window only behind `mw_desk()`), `wording` ("chip", old names of Private) and `emoji` (only the thumbs in texts) (#7262).

### Changed
- Request address: a mail over the hourly limit now waits read in the mailbox until the window is over instead of being fetched whole again every minute; at most 20 waiting mails come back per round, a project holds at most 200 waiting mails a window, and one more is not taken (it stays read in the mailbox) - the project's owner and admins get the number in the News (#6874).
- Search hits (also `kind=all`) and the lists of contacts, events, knowledge pages and knowledge files name their author (`created_by`, a message hit `author_id`, a knowledge file `by`); the MCP data fence judges them and fences a hit's `snippet` of another account (#7192).
- `tools/check_suppress.py` also counts the file-wide `# ruff: noqa` / `# flake8: noqa`, `type: ignore`, `nosec`, `@ts-ignore` / `@ts-nocheck` and markers inside JavaScript block comments over several lines; six dead `type: ignore` are gone (#7176).

### Fixed
- A file of a mail, a reply by mail, a request mail, a public form or a documents mail that is left out (storage full, virus scanner, a list locked in the browser, over the size limit) no longer disappears silently: the task says which file and why, the owner gets a News item, and the mail stays in the mailbox (#6531).
- `tools/check_globals.py` says why and what instead like every check (the suite p21403_c_suppress was red on main) (#7176).
