Package 2.1491 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Quotations and invoices: "Add service" with nothing to choose yet (no service in the catalogue, no part) adds a free position at once with the cursor in its field "Service" instead of an empty sheet; while there is no service, a small line under the positions leads to Settings > Templates > Services (#7596)
- Quotations, invoices and the other drafts: the row of buttons of the head (next step, Finalise, Preview, Download, "…") stands once more under the sums - the same buttons, rights and locks; none for readers or finalised documents (#7605)

### Fixed
- Office lists: a list load still running when the list was discarded (a new quotation, another tab's change) no longer puts its older answer over it - the list loads again; a state push during a load is kept, not dropped (#7574)
- Files: "Public link… > Create link" and "Share…" in the view of a plain file (not a recognised document) no longer show "Not found" over the new link - the view reads the file again over its file-store entry (#7590)
- Files under "All workspaces": the overview no longer shows a round "Upload" (+) that did nothing - it kept the button of the space shown before; a space or folder has Upload with its file field as before (#7611)
