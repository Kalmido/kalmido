Package 2.1264 (Bündel: Sicherheit: Konto-Sperre (Admin/Betreiber) pausier, Datenschutz: Benachrichtigungs-Mails mit Inhalten , Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne P).
### Changed
- Security: a locked account locks its personal agents too. Their API tokens answer 403 `owner_locked`, the hosted MCP answers 403 (no refresh), waiting webhook deliveries are dropped and no new events are queued; nothing is written to the agent, so unlocking the account lets it work again at once. Team agents (no owner) are not affected (#6351).
- Privacy: e-mails with content (daily summary, e-mails for single events, "Send now") go only to a confirmed address. An unconfirmed address gets the confirmation e-mail once instead, with a line why; Settings > Notifications shows a hint with the button "Confirm address" next to both e-mail switches. The "address changed" warning goes to the old address only (#6352).
- Office: finalising a document and setting its number check the version of the draft shown (`expect_updated_at`); when the draft was changed on another device the conflict bar "Reload" appears and nothing is finalised or numbered (#6331).
### Fixed
- A task open in the detail panel (always on a phone) follows a change made on another device, and typing on no longer overwrites that change silently: the conflict check builds on the text the field showed (#6326).
- Live updates on a second device: a late delta answer no longer overwrites newer tasks, new and changed notes arrive (notes are a light area of their own), an open calendar / Today view draws events changed elsewhere, a team chat signal that arrives while typing is not lost, an open storage folder reloads and "Today" moves on at midnight (#6335; a focused select field still holds updates back, that part follows).
