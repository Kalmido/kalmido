Package 2.1461 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher), Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Added
- #6626: The status link of a customer report (/p/...) ends: 90 days after the task was done (reopening makes it valid again), or at once when the team ends it in the task's box "Client report" ("End the status link", with undo). "Send a new status link" makes a new link (the old one stops working at once) and mails it to the client's address. The box says until when the link is valid, or that it ended / expired; an ended or expired link shows the same "not found" page as every unknown link, sends no answer mails, and is no link in the client's portal list.

### Changed
- #7190: A project's PO number is taken from a document's order reference only by the project's owner or admins; a changed project PO updates only the drafts of offices where the person has a role, each change in the office log; the project's PO in the document editor only for people who see the project. A background job's permission question in the agent chat is answered by its button or a reply to it, never by a bare "ok" / "yes"; the docs of the jobs' permission channel say what it trusts.
- #6643: Agents: tasks from the inbound webhook, a form or an error report made before 2.1269 get their outside origin (untrusted) once at the update; a task moved with the account move keeps its origin; a comment of an account deleted since is marked `former_account` (was `public_link`), still untrusted; a reply by e-mail of a sender without an account is `mail`.

### Fixed
- #6494: The admin alert names every part of the watchdog in plain words (invoice series, dunning, subscriptions, payment notices, schedules, list rules and more, in every language) instead of its raw English name; a test is red when a new part has no name.
- #6637: An open invoice with a rest below 1.00 (a final invoice after rounded instalments: 0.01 VAT) is no longer "overdue" and gets no reminder suggestion or overdue notice; it stays open. Marking reminder levels of the same invoice as paid counts the highest interest and flat fee of the paid levels once, whatever the order of the marks.
