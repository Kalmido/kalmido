- A click (or Enter) on the Kalmido logo opens the settings - in the head bar and at the top of the phone's sidebar (was: Start); Start stays in the account menu and the command palette (#7526)
- Private wears its colour in every area as soon as you have an organisation - one rule in the core for rows, open objects and overviews; the Files overview shows Private's stripe like the office (#7661)

- Every opened object (knowledge page, table, folder, document, address book) has the text button "Share" in its head, also before the first share; it opens the area's share dialog (#7015)
- One word each: "History" (was "Recent changes" / "Details and history"), the contact card's "Activities", the document tab "Sharing" (was "Access"), "Download" for the thing itself and "Export as …" for another format, "Encrypt folder…" / "Encryption…" (was "Lock folder…") (#7015)
- The document head: Move to the trash is its red symbol (label and title stay); on a phone "Actions" is the ⋯ (#7015)
