Package 2.1454 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher), Felder am Handy: Schrift in Eingabefeldern und Dro).

### Added
- #7395: Back and Forward as two arrows in the header, right before the bell (computer and tablet; on a phone off by default, switch in Settings > Appearance "Back and forward arrows in the header"). Forward also with Alt+→ / ⌘] and the mouse's Forward button; both keys in the keyboard shortcut overview. The single Back arrow left of the page title is gone.

### Changed
- #7409: On a phone the text in fields and dropdowns is as large as the label next to it (16 px only on iPhone / iPad, against Safari's zoom); in dialogs on a phone dropdowns and number fields are only as wide as their content.
- #7180: install.sh: a local build of a release tag (KALMIDO_BUILD=1) carries the version and shell cache number of its tag (tools/stamp_release.py --nur-version, needs python3).

### Fixed
- #7399: Clients on a computer: a click on a client swaps only the card - the list, its search field and bar stay where they are (scroll position kept, also when coming back to a client seen before); the card on screen stays dimmed until the next client is loaded instead of "Loading…", and the page draws twice instead of 8-10 times.
- #7407: "New client" under "All workspaces": "Belongs to (main client)" offers only the main clients of the chosen workspace (empty and locked until one is chosen, a main client of another workspace is cleared on a change), and the message "Select the workspace" goes away once one is chosen.
