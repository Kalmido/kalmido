Package 2.1222 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).
### Added
- #4566: Packing list per shoot day - taking a quotation into the project books the devices of each shoot part's equipment set as pencilled-in bookings on the shoot day (with quantity, the usual double-booking warning; a new quotation version adapts them like the crew places); the call sheet shows them as a packing list (device, category, quantity, tickable, saved with the call sheet, also in the PDF), items without a device of the master data as text lines. New column hub_bookings.qty (default 1, backwards compatible).
- #4566: Post-calculation - the button "Post-calculation" on an accepted quotation with tasks shows planned against actual hours per position and makes an invoice draft in which hour positions linked to tasks carry the tracked billable, not yet billed hours (rounded to 0.25 h; rate from the quotation); the counted times are bound to the draft (deleting it frees them). Nothing becomes final by itself.
- #5525: A wiki page as a presentation - "Present" first in the page's "…" menu (also for readers): every # / ## heading starts a slide, text before the first heading becomes a title slide; full screen, arrow keys, swipe, Esc / Back to leave.
### Changed
- #6180: Office and clients wear the organisation's colour - the office badge and every office page (also an open quotation, invoice, receipt or template) mark the organisation, the area overview shows each organisation's stripe, client rows carry the stripe and their origin under "All", the open client card is marked. Private stays neutral.
