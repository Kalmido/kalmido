Package 2.1486.0 (Bündel: Weich rot: p2450_b_ui, Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Settings: a section is open at the start unless it is long (higher than 320 px on a phone, measured by a test) or rarely needed / delicate (reset, delete account, server, sync, storage, active sessions, import / export / move, sample data); the first section of every tab is always open (Account: Profile), Company & office opens its first group. What you opened or closed yourself still wins. Every section has its own icon (Account showed the person icon on almost every row) (#7555)
- The remaining plain requests of the web app (sign-in, invitations, agents, scans, imports, PDFs, downloads, probes) have the shared time limit: no answer = a message instead of waiting for ever; the few that stay plain say why (#7521)

### Fixed
- Offline start on iOS (Safari / home screen app): when the browser's database does not answer at a start, the app no longer stays on a blank page - after a few seconds it starts from the local copy, shows "Server not reachable" with Try again, or loads from the server once it is back (#7549)
- Moving a client out of an organisation: a member without the right gets the 403 before the note about finalised documents, so nothing of the office is named (#7408)
- Test p2450_b_ui: reads the server key warning by its text; the test mode's warning stands first since #6871 (#7578)
