Package 2.1504 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Changed
- Bank: taking a suggestion, a mark, a split, taking back, "without receipt" and the walk-through show the booking's new state at once; the answer only updates that row and the counters, the list is not reloaded and keeps its scroll position; an error or a missing answer brings the row back with "Not saved - Try again"; a split keeps the typed amounts on an error; an upload whose matching failed matches the already uploaded file on "Try again"; the walk-through shows the next entry at once and never hangs (#7497)
- Credit cards: matching a position (receipt, no receipt needed, private, take back, upload) shows at once; the answer updates only that position and the statement's figures, no reload of cards and statement after each click (#7499)
- Core "Write shows at once" (rwRows): W.act for a single action on a list row; Ctrl+Z waits for actions still on their way (#7497, #7499)
- Saving quotation / invoice positions writes only changed positions, reads the catalogue and the positions once and can answer short ({lite: true}); a field change costs the same number of database statements at 50 and 200 positions (#7505)
- Starter dispatcher: the day report names why each skipped task was not built and that "Confirm as an order" makes it buildable; self-test of the confirmation cases (#7529)

### Fixed
- Mailbox outage mail: one mail per 3 days per workspace and mailbox address, also after removing and re-adding the mailbox; network failures wait at most 15 min (only a refused login backs off up to 6 h); the mailbox name in notices is cut to 60 characters (#6905)
