Package 2.1494 (Bündel: Aufgabe als Auftrag bestätigen oder ablehnen: das , Gegenlesen job/5922: 2 offene Funde nach allen Run, Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne P).

### Added
- Confirm a task as an order or reject it: project owners, project admins and admins see "Confirm…" in the task panel's header of an open task someone else wrote (on a phone: on top of the task's "…" menu) with "Confirm as an order" and "Reject…". The panel shows "Not confirmed as an order" / "Confirmed as an order" with name and time (and "Withdraw"); "Template not filled in: …" names empty ticket-template sections. The yes is bound to title + description: only the confirmer's own direct edit (the task panel, the multi-select edit) carries it to the new text; every other change of the text - by someone else, a token, an applied agent proposal, a merge, undo / redo - ends it with a history line. "Reject…" needs a reason and closes the task as won't do. The API shows `confirmed {by, at}` read only; agents, tokens, child accounts and support sessions can neither confirm nor reject. One shared core block (`kalmido/core/boundyes.py`) carries this yes and the yes to a one-way step (#7528, #7588).
- "Waiting for me" can be worked through: a row opens its entry in the view (the link to its target stays in the entry) with the buttons of the target - Approve / Reject of a job, Yes / No of an approval card without a step, Approve / Back of a review, the yes to a one-way step, Confirm as an order / Reject. After a decision (here, on the target or on another device) the next entry opens by itself; at the end "Nothing waits for you any more". On a computer the list stands left and the entry right; on a phone the entry fills the view with "List", "Back" and "Skip". Keys: j / k next and back, y / n only where an entry has exactly yes and no (never the one-way yes, never a step that needs a text) (#7538).
- "Waiting for me" has the group "Orders to confirm": open tasks another person's agent wrote into a project you decide, sorted by sender; your own agents' tasks do not wait there. The API's `/me/waiting` names the kind `confirm`, always empty for agents and tokens (#7538).
- Switching off Settings > Modules > Office & finance > "Also private" while invoice series or receipt reminders keep running in the private office now also leaves a bell entry ("Still running in your private office: …"); tapping it opens Settings > Modules. Nothing is stopped (#7392).

### Changed
- Settings > Company & office has its own office tabs on top (Private, each organisation; symbol and colour of the workspace): a tab changes only whose office settings the page shows and saves - the app's workspace stays as it is, "All workspaces" too. Preselected: the office page you came from, else the current workspace, else the office used last there. With one office there are no tabs, only its name. The workspace buttons under "Which workspace?" and the "Switch to …" hint are gone from the settings (#7556).
- The yes to a one-way step uses the same decider rule as the confirmation (also refused for child accounts, support sessions and tokens) (#7528).

### Fixed
- A member whose module card in an organisation has the office but not Documents could not upload or open receipts in that office (403); the office on the card now carries its document calls, the documents list only while it shows this organisation's office (#7392).

One-way: migration `kalmido/core/migrations.d/7528.py` adds `tasks.confirmed_by`, `confirmed_at`, `confirmed_sig` and a partial index; an older version ignores them, but its writes of title or description leave the text state behind, so confirmations given until then read as ended after a step back.
