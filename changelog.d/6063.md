Package 2.1501.0 (Bündel: Büro Handy: 'Beleg fotografieren' und 'Dateien hoc, Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher), Meine Aufgaben: eigene Abschnitte).

### Added
- Sign-offs (Approvals): every comment (point, area, timecode) has "Task from it" - one click makes a task in the sign-off's project with the comment as its title and the sign-off, version and place in its description; the comment then shows the task and whether it is open or done, the task's panel shows "From the sign-off …" (opens it). Only people who may change the sign-off and write in the project; a sign-off only at a client offers nothing; an agent proposes it, a person clicks. The connection is a link of the shared core (rel review_task) (#7533)
- My tasks: your own sections have the grip on their head and drag to a new order like a project's sections (the same drag, saved for My tasks; "Recently assigned" stays on top); the order stays after a reload (#7518)
- Starter dispatcher: KALMIDO_DISPATCH_LAND=local for projects that integrate only on their computer - after the green check nothing is pushed and no pull request is opened; the card "Ready to integrate" names the branch job/<id>, the checked commit and the target branch, the reading job reads the branch; approved_cmd gets {sha}; without the switch everything stays as before. docs/AGENTS.md describes both ways and that a topic project's base branch is the branch checked out in its folder (#7572)

### Changed
- Knowledge on a computer and a tablet: an open page scrolls inside again - the card ends at the window's edge, "Files" and the footer stay in sight; "Files" and "Linked from" are one folded line each below the text (with their count, opened on demand; a phone shows them open as before) (#7658)
- Section menus: "Move up" / "Move down" stay in a section's menu also on touch screens, beside the drag (#7518)

### Fixed
- Office on a phone: "Photograph receipt" and "Upload files" stand side by side in one line (half the width each) everywhere - bookkeeping, the receipts list, the empty receipts view and a member's "Submit receipt"; only when a label does not fit (320 px) they wrap (#7689)
