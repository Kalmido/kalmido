Package 2.1462 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Added
- Office: "Finalise and send all checked" for drafts of invoice issuers without a mark (list filtered by issuer): each checked draft runs the existing finalise and send, drafts with a mark (new client, look-alike, hold, reverse charge …) stay single with their reason; the result is shown per draft (#7249)
- Tables: the cell details beside a table get wider by dragging the grip between table and side column (at least 14rem, default 17.5rem, the table keeps 14rem; double-click resets; not on phones) (#7305)

### Changed
- Project lists: dragging a single task (before / after a row, onto a section head or kanban column, onto "New section") runs through the same drop core as a selection; every gesture is one undo step, also pure reordering; a locked task shows "Unlock" (#7351)
- The sidebar folded into a drawer (narrow window or open task) takes the remembered sidebar width and has the same grip as the fixed sidebar (at most 80 % of the window; phone drawer unchanged) (#7307)

### Fixed
- Tab switches outside the office no longer flicker or jump to the top: time tracking period and scope, team tabs (workload / absences / dispatch), review period and mood statistics, a table's sheets, the client card's month - the tab row stays, the active tab switches at once, the last content waits dimmed and the view is drawn once (#7398)
