### Changed
- Team chat: the message line is calmer - on a touch screen the smiley and the "..." menu appear after a tap on the message; read ticks and "edited" sit in the time row; a reaction shows at once and is taken back if the server refuses (#6096).
- Files: the file view shows a preview for every kind of file in its preview column - Word / Excel as the office preview, text as text, other kinds with their icon, "No preview for this kind of file" and Download; the zoom bar only where there are pages; Back names the folder you came from; on a phone the title stands whole in a row of its own (#6110).
- Start page on computer and tablet: the title is "Start" beside the sidebar, two columns without holes at 820 px, entries from another workspace carry its colour dot and name; the calendar on a touch tablet shows one main button (#6126).
- Notice bars: offline is one sign (the header chip in words, click to retry), the bottom bar only while changes wait; "New version available", upload bars and the code hint share one stack that covers nothing and "New version" can be closed; the code hint is for server admins only (#6132).
- Lists at a middle width or with a task open: the archive card puts its buttons under the name, the multi-select bar sits at the bottom in the list's width with readable type, the list calendar's bar and the task's breadcrumb wrap instead of being squeezed (#6140).
