Package 2.1507 (Bündel: Sammelpaket: Aufgaben ohne Paket (Dispatcher)).

### Fixed
- Organisation roles: a person moved down to guest, accounting or read-only no longer keeps owning or managing the organisation's projects, calendars and address books - what they own goes to the admin making the change ("Change role" names it first), they keep editing (read-only: reading); one table of what a role may hold caps every request, also for older data, and "Clean up" in the boundary check hands such leftovers to the first admin; leaving and a role down take one way (#6610)
- Organisation invitations end when the inviting person loses the admin right, leaves, is locked, sets a new password or switches on a second factor (the password and two-factor dialogs say so); every kind of invitation then stays in the members list as expired with "Send again", and accepting checks the inviter again; the other admins are told when someone joins as admin (#6297)
- Organisation members: a role is moved down only in the members list, which first names what goes to whom (also for one's own role and for saved views); adding an address that is already a member never lowers its role (#6610)
- Security hardening (audit #4741): a text starting like a sealed value no longer breaks its row; restoring a comment or team chat message follows the write rules and a moderator's delete; a read-only member's mail reply with a forged sender makes no comment; another person's own ticket types show only as their projects offer them; only admins delete others' tables and pages from an organisation's trash for good; an office upload needs the receipts right; the split view no longer overwrites the offline app page; the innerHTML check sees concatenations and bare tr() values (#6297)

### Changed
- Agents: every kind of the shared core names its token scopes (tables, files, knowledge pages, shooting days and document views need their area's scope also for links), every file download needs attachments:read, and a run with web research sends only fields without free text without an approval card (#6297)
